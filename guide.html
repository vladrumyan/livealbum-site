Live Album guide

How to make an NFC photo album that plays videos, voice notes and music, which stickers to buy, and answers to common questions. The same guide is in the app: tap the ? icon.

How to make an NFC photo album

Which NFC stickers to use

Any NTAG213, NTAG215 or NTAG216 NFC sticker works. NTAG213 is plenty. Round stickers of 25–30 mm fit behind most photos.

Where to put them

Behind the middle of the photo works best. Keep each sticker away from the edge of the page and from other stickers.

Stop the wrong page playing (shielding)

Phones can read a sticker through several pages. Put a ferrite sheet and then a piece of aluminium foil behind each sticker (for example glued between two pages), so only the page you hold your phone over plays.

Test every page

After linking, close the app and hold your phone over each photo once. If a page doesn't play, link its sticker again.

Find your phone's NFC spot

Most phones read stickers near the middle or top of the back. Thick or metal phone cases can stop them reading.

Questions

Nothing happens when I hold my phone over a photo

Check that NFC is on in your phone's settings, take off a thick case, and hold the phone still over the photo for a second or two. Try moving it slowly to find your phone's NFC spot.

The wrong page plays

Your phone is reading a sticker on another page. Put a ferrite sheet and aluminium foil behind each sticker (see How to make an NFC photo album).

My phone doesn't have NFC

You can still make albums and play them: open an album and tap a page to play it. Linking and reading stickers needs a phone with NFC.

How do I give an album to someone?

Open the album's menu (⋮) and choose “Export album” to send it in WhatsApp, email or any other app, or “Send to a nearby phone” if they're next to you. They need an Android phone with NFC and the free Live Album app. The same stickers then play on their phone.

What do I get with Pro?

Free gives you one album with up to 5 pages, with every page type. Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks unlimited albums and pages. Playing albums you've been given is always free.

A sticker is lost or damaged

Stick a new one in its place, open “Edit pages” and link it to the same page.

Can I change what a page plays after printing?

Yes. Edit the page in the app: the sticker stays the same and plays the new video, photos or audio.

Does it need the internet?

No. Everything is stored on your phone, except pages that play online videos such as YouTube.

Where are my photos and videos kept?

Only on your phone. Live Album has no account, no ads and no tracking. To keep a backup, export an album and save the file somewhere safe, such as Google Drive.

Where to buy NFC stickers

Look for NTAG213 NFC stickers (round, 25–30 mm), thin ferrite sheets and aluminium foil. They're sold in packs by many shops.
